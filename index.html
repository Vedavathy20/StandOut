About the Event

Where Developers Shape Tomorrow

StandOut 2026 is a two-day immersive experience designed for developers, architects, and tech leaders who refuse to stand still. From deep-dive workshops to visionary keynotes, every session is crafted to push boundaries.

Now in its fifth edition, StandOut has grown from a 200-person meetup into one of Asia's most anticipated developer gatherings — attracting speakers from Google, Meta, CERN, and dozens of pioneering startups.
